Questions owners ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Sônia in English, in the dashboard or on Telegram, and she coordinates the rest. Every screen in the dashboard has a step-by-step guide.

Does Sônia do things without asking me?

Only routine work. Proposals, posts, bulk messages, bookings and any spending wait for your OK. The rules are agreed with you during setup and can change later.

Is it a fit for my type of business?

The team covers what almost every business needs: marketing, sales, customer service and operations. If you want to confirm your case, book a call before you subscribe.

What's not included in the price?

The AI model subscription, paid directly to the provider (ChatGPT starts at about US$ 20 a month), and any other tool you choose to connect.

What if the AI provider goes down?

Sônia depends on AI providers such as ChatGPT and on your subscription's quota. If a provider is down or the quota runs out, she can't respond until the service is back. That's why we don't promise uninterrupted operation.

Is there a guarantee?

Yes. If within 30 days of payment you decide not to continue, we refund 100% of what you paid. Just ask by email.
